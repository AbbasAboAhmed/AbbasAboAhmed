### About me

Data Engineer with 3 years of experience designing data warehouses, building ETL pipelines with Databricks, PySpark and Azure, and delivering Power BI reporting.

- Currently a **Data Analytics Engineer at Santechture**, working with healthcare revenue cycle (RCM) data.
- Before that: data engineering and BI roles at **Unilever** and **Teleperformance**.
- An accounting background helps me turn complex data into reliable, decision-ready insights.
- I explain data engineering in Arabic on my YouTube channel.
